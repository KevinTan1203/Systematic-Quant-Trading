Margin Trading Simulator

Day-by-day mark-to-market · Margin calls · Equity & maintenance levels

Simulation Parameters

You buy shares using margin. The broker lends (1 − Initial Margin). Each day the position is marked-to-market. If Equity / Market Value falls below Maintenance Margin → Margin Call. The “Cash Required” column shows how much you must deposit to bring equity back to the Initial Margin level.
