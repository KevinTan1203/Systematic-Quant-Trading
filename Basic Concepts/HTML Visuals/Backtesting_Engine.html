07Overfitting & Diagnostics
In-sample vs out-of-sample

Tuning parameters while looking at the full history contaminates evaluation. This engine reports metrics on a holdout segment that was not used to “choose” the displayed path’s narrative — but if you manually hunt for parameters that maximise OOS too, you re-overfit. Treat OOS as a single test, not a second training set.

Parameter sensitivity

Robust rules degrade gracefully when parameters shift slightly. A razor-thin peak in the sensitivity chart often means the result is noise. Prefer plateaus over spikes.

What this engine does not model

No order-book microstructure, partial fills, corporate actions, or regime-conditional short borrow costs. Synthetic paths lack structural breaks you will meet in live markets.

Educational backtesting platform. Synthetic OHLCV. Sequential fill model with explicit lag. Not investment advice; not a production research stack.
