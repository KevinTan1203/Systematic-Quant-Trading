No guaranteed edge. A positive IV − RV gap is not a trading signal by itself. Realized vol is backward-looking; IV embeds risk premia, demand for hedges, jump risk and market structure. Model risk, path dependence, transaction costs and surface dynamics can erase apparent “richness” or “cheapness.”

07Why IV − RV Is Not a Trade Ticket
Realized-vol uncertainty

RV is a sample from a path that has already occurred (or a forecast model). Future realized volatility can differ sharply — especially around events. Selling “rich” IV when RV was low can still lose if vol spikes.

Model risk & surface dynamics

BS Greeks assume a flat vol world; real P&L depends on the full surface move (sticky strike vs sticky delta, skew dynamics). A scenario that only bumps ATM can misstate option P&L when skew steepens simultaneously.

Transaction costs & carry

Crossing bid–ask on wings, margin, and the path of delta-hedging costs all erode theoretical edges. Apparent calendar or butterfly richness may not cover these frictions.
