09Explicit vs Implicit Costs
Explicit costs

Commissions, exchange fees, taxes and clear, invoiceable charges. They are easy to measure and often small relative to market impact on large orders — but they still belong in a full TCA.

Implicit costs

Spread paid when demanding liquidity, market impact from your own size, delay/timing costs as the mid moves, and opportunity cost on size you never filled. These dominate institutional TCA and require a benchmark (arrival, VWAP, close, etc.) to estimate.

Why minimising one cost can raise another

Trading slower reduces impact but increases timing and opportunity risk if the market runs away. Trading faster locks in price but walks the book and pays more impact and spread. Passive limit strategies may earn the spread when they fill — and incur large opportunity cost when they do not. TCA is about the trade-off surface, not a single cost line item.
