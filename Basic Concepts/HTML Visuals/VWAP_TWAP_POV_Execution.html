07Formula Reference
Implementation Shortfall (IS)
IS_buy = AvgExecPx − ArrivalPx · IS_sell = ArrivalPx − AvgExecPx
IS_bps = IS / ArrivalPx × 10 000
Arrival price is the mid (or decision price) when the order is released. Positive IS means underperformance versus doing nothing at arrival.
Market VWAP & Your VWAP
Market VWAP = Σ (P_t · V_t) / Σ V_t · Your VWAP = Σ (P_fill · q_fill) / Σ q_fill
Outperformance vs market VWAP is a common agent benchmark; it is not the same as implementation shortfall.
TWAP / VWAP / POV Schedules
TWAP: q_t = Q / N
VWAP: q_t = Q · (E[V_t] / Σ E[V])
POV: q_t = min( remaining, pov% · V_t )
N = number of buckets. Expected volume curve is the chosen profile scaled to ADV over the session fraction.
Temporary Market Impact (synthetic)
ΔP_impact ≈ sign · impactCoeff · σ · (q_t / V_t)^0.5 · P
Square-root impact on participation in the bucket. Temporary impact mean-reverts partially in subsequent buckets; permanent fraction is smaller.
08Execution Concepts
TWAP vs VWAP vs POV

TWAP slices the parent into equal quantities per time bucket. Simple and robust when you do not trust the volume forecast; can overweight thin periods and underweight the close.

VWAP targets the market’s volume-weighted average price by scheduling size proportional to an expected volume curve (here: U-shape, flat, front- or back-loaded). It minimises tracking error to market VWAP when the forecast is accurate.

POV (percentage of volume) trades as a fixed fraction of observed market volume each bucket. It automatically slows in quiet periods and speeds up when volume appears — completion is path-dependent.

Implementation shortfall & cost components

IS decomposes conceptually into spread / instantaneous cost, market impact (your own trading moving the price), and timing / opportunity cost (drift of the mid while you are still executing). Aggressive strategies pay more impact but less timing risk; passive strategies do the opposite.

Urgency, impact & timing risk

Higher urgency increases the fraction of each slice that crosses the spread and the effective participation, raising impact but finishing earlier. In a rising market, a slow buy order suffers opportunity cost; in a falling market, patience is rewarded. Volatility amplifies both impact uncertainty and timing risk.

Why this market is synthetic

Price paths, volume profiles, shocks and impact are generated from stylised models chosen for pedagogical clarity. They illustrate mechanisms — they are not calibrated to a specific venue or security, and must not be read as forecasts or as evidence of strategy performance in live markets.
