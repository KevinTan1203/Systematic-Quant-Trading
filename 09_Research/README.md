# 09_Research

This section supports ongoing learning and original research through curated academic and practitioner resources.

## Structure

### 1. Papers

Collection of key academic and industry papers (see nested README for organisation and recommended reading order).

### 2. Research Notes

Personal or curated notes on important topics, derivations, and insights (see nested README).

### 3. Literature Reviews

Synthesised reviews of major research areas (see nested README).

## Learning Objectives

- Engage directly with primary literature.
- Build the habit of critical reading and note-taking.
- Develop the ability to synthesise findings across papers.
- Maintain an organised personal research library.

Use this section in parallel with active projects and interview preparation. Regular engagement with the literature is a hallmark of strong quant researchers.
