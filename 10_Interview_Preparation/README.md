# 10_Interview_Preparation

Targeted preparation materials for quantitative research, development, and trading interviews.

## Structure

### 1. Quant Research

Interview questions, case studies, and frameworks specific to research roles (see nested README).

### 2. Quant Development

Software engineering, system design, and coding challenges relevant to quant dev positions (see nested README).

### 3. Quant Trading

- Crypto Trading
  - Crypto Trading Interview.ipynb
  - Crypto-Web3-Handbook.ipynb
  - Proprietary Trading Interview Preparation.ipynb
- Supporting README covering trading interviews more broadly.

### 4. Probability

Classic probability puzzles and interview problems (see nested README).

### 5. Mental Math

Speed and accuracy drills for quantitative interviews (see nested README).

### 6. Market Microstructure

Microstructure questions commonly asked in trading and research interviews (see nested README).

### 7. Resume Optimisation

Guidance on structuring and refining quant resumes (see nested README).

## Learning Objectives

- Master the core technical questions asked in quant interviews.
- Develop clear communication of complex ideas.
- Build confidence with probability, mental math, and microstructure topics.
- Prepare role-specific materials (research, development, trading, crypto).
- Present experience effectively through a strong resume.

Practice actively. Interview success depends on both knowledge and the ability to think and communicate under pressure.
