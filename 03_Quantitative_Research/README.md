This section focuses on the end-to-end process of generating, testing, and validating trading ideas and investment strategies. It covers alpha research, factor modelling, statistical arbitrage, portfolio construction, rigorous backtesting, and machine learning applications.

## Learning Objectives

- Design and evaluate alpha signals systematically.
- Build and interpret factor models.
- Implement and stress-test statistical arbitrage strategies.
- Construct portfolios that respect realistic constraints.
- Conduct rigorous, bias-aware backtests and validation.
- Integrate machine learning thoughtfully into the research process.
- Account for execution realities early in the research pipeline.

This section emphasises process discipline. Always prioritise validation and model risk awareness over raw performance metrics.
