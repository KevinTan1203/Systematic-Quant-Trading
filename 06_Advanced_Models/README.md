### 9. Advanced Machine Learning

Sophisticated ML architectures and techniques for financial applications (see nested README).

## Learning Objectives

- Master the mathematical machinery of modern quantitative finance.
- Apply stochastic processes and calculus to pricing and risk problems.
- Use Monte Carlo, state-space, HMM, and Kalman methods for inference and simulation.
- Model extreme events and complex dependence structures.
- Deploy advanced machine learning thoughtfully in a financial context.

This material is mathematically demanding. Ensure strong foundations from Section 01 before proceeding.
