Quick Insight

When λ = 0 the model collapses to classic geometric Brownian motion. Raising λ + making μJ negative produces the sudden crashes seen in real markets (Flash Crash, COVID crash, earnings gaps…).

The Merton Jump-Diffusion SDE

Continuous Brownian motion + rare discontinuous jumps (Poisson arrivals). The −λκ term compensates the drift so that the expected return stays μ.
