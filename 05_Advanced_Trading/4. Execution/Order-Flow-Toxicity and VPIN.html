What am I looking at?
Reservation r = Mid × (1 − skew_bps/10,000), skew_bps = (q/qsize) × volatility-bps-component — long inventory shifts r below mid (encourages selling). Dynamic half-spread = Base (live observed half-spread) + Volatility + Toxicity + Liquidity components, each expressed natively in bps so the result never depends on the asset's absolute price. Final Bid/Ask = Dynamic Bid/Ask extended further out by your Custom bps margin — this is what the paper simulator actually trades against.

Model Mathematics
Observed
S = (bid+ask)/2 · spread_bps = (ask-bid)/S × 10000
OBI = (V_bid−V_ask)/(V_bid+V_ask)
r_t = ln(S_t/S_{t-1}) · realised vol from rolling returns (annualised × √(periods/yr) using the median observed sampling interval)

VPIN (toxicity proxy)
VPIN = Σ|V_buy,i − V_sell,i| / (n × V_bucket) over completed equal-volume buckets

Dynamic half-spread (bps-native, unit-safe)
σ_bps = σ_ann / √(seconds/yr) × 10000 (fractional annualised vol expressed in bps/√s)
vol_bps = √γ × σ_bps × √τ
tox_bps = λ_bps × f(VPIN)  (λ_bps is a dashboard-defined bps-at-VPIN=1 coefficient)
liq_bps = min(80, (qsize·mid / top-of-book depth notional) × 10 × κ)
base_bps = max(0, observed_spread_bps / 2)
dynHalf_bps = min(base_bps + vol_bps + tox_bps + liq_bps, maxHalfSpreadBps)

Reservation price & model quote
skew_bps = (q / qsize) × vol_bps   r = mid × (1 − skew_bps/10000)
dynBid = r × (1 − dynHalf_bps/10000)   dynAsk = r × (1 + dynHalf_bps/10000)

Custom margin & final paper quote
customBid = mid × (1 − bidMarginBps/10000)   customAsk = mid × (1 + askMarginBps/10000)
finalBid = dynBid − mid × bidMarginBps/10000   finalAsk = dynAsk + mid × askMarginBps/10000
