Bond Mathematics

▸
Bond Price P = Σ CFt / (1+y/m)t Periodic Coupon C = (F × c) / m Current Yield Annual Coupon / Price Macaulay Duration (years) DM = [Σ t × PV(CFt)] / (P × m) Modified Duration DMod = DM / (1 + y/m) Convexity Conv = (1/P) × Σ [ CFt × t(t+1) / (m² (1+y/m)t+2) ] Approx. Price Change ΔP/P ≈ −DMod Δy + ½ Conv (Δy)²

All metrics are derived from the same cash-flow engine. Frequency changes n = years × m and the periodic coupon.
