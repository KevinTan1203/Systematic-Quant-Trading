04 / Scaling rules

Annualisation & Volatility Scaling Calculator

Different rules apply: compounding (or (1+μ)^N−1) for simple returns, multiplication by N for log returns, and √N for volatility. N must match data frequency.

06 / Limits of diversification

Diversification Benefit & Systematic-Risk Floor

Equal-weight portfolio volatility falls with N but asymptotes to a floor set by average pairwise correlation (systematic risk that cannot be diversified away).
