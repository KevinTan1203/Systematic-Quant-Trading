Surface Model Specification

F = S · e^{(r−q)T}
m = ln(K / F)
ATM(T) = ATM₀ + slope · √T
IV(K,T) = clip[ ATM(T) + β₁·T^(−ν/2)·m + β₂·T^(−ν/2)·m² , σ_min, σ_max ]
Forward moneyness anchors the smile to the forward. Time decay of skew/curvature follows a power law. Prices use Black–Scholes–Merton with continuous yield q. This is a teaching terminal, not a production vol-surface stack.
Terminal notes — reading the surface

The surface maps implied volatility to strike and maturity. Traders read skew (left–right tilt), smile (wings), and term structure (front vs back) as a compact summary of distributional risk premia.

Black–Scholes price & Greeks on a local IV

Each (K,T) is valued with a single σ = IV(K,T) inside BSM. That is the definition of Black implied vol. Delta/Gamma/Vega/Theta/Rho shown here are pure BSM Greeks at that local σ — not sticky-delta or model-free dual deltas.

Why this is labelled synthetic
  • No exchange feed, no NBBO, no calibration to listed quotes.
  • No butterfly/calendar arbitrage projection.
  • Bid/ask in the chain are cosmetic spreads around model mid.
